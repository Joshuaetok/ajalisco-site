From Source to Seal

Every sachet passes our multi-stage protocol

Our water is drawn from certified boreholes through stainless steel pipes, purified, lab-tested and sealed in food-grade film in a hygienic environment, before it ever reaches you.

NAFDAC Approved Lab tested Food-grade packaging
  1. Sourcing

    Certified boreholes & stainless steel pipes

  2. Filtration & RO

    Sand, carbon, microfiltration & reverse osmosis

  3. Lab Testing

    Checked for pH, TDS and microbes

  4. UV & Ozonation

    Final disinfection for total purity

  5. Hygienic Packaging

    Sealed in food-grade film, ready for you
