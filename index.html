Ajalisco Table Water

Clean water,
sealed fresh.

Every sachet goes through a six-stage purification process and is sealed on-site, so it arrives cool, clean and tasting of nothing but water.

Bag of sachet water

For homes, offices, sites & events

  1. 01Sand filtration

    Removes sediment and larger particles.

  2. 02Carbon filtration

    Clears odours, chlorine and off-tastes.

  3. 03Microfiltration

    Traps fine particles down to microns.

  4. 04Reverse osmosis

    Strips out dissolved impurities.

  5. 05UV treatment

    Neutralises bacteria and viruses.

  6. 06Ozonation

    Final disinfection before sealing.
