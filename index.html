About Us

Pure quality, delivered fresh — since 2025

Our Humble Beginnings

Our Journey in the Production Industry

Founded in 2025, Ajalisco started as a humble sachet water factory in Nigeria, driven by a big dream to provide quality hydration. Today, we have grown into a trusted brand known for delivering clean water and wholesome bread. Our products are crafted with honesty, hygiene, and care, dedicated to serving our community with integrity. Our continuous growth reflects our unwavering commitment to quality and the well-being of the people we serve.

Mission

To produce safe, high-quality consumables—including sachet water and wholesome bread—that nourish and delight families across Nigeria.

Vision

To be Nigeria’s most trusted producer of high-quality, affordable, and nutritious products—delivering refreshment, health, and happiness to every home. We aspire to expand our reach, uphold the highest standards of hygiene and integrity, and positively impact the communities we serve through innovation and excellence.

Values

Our Core Values

Quality & Safety

We are committed to producing products that meet the highest standards of safety, hygiene, and quality to ensure our customers receive only the best.

Honesty & Transparency

Honesty and transparency guide everything we do, from sourcing ingredients to delivering our products to your hands.

Customer Focus

Our customers are at the heart of our business. We strive to exceed expectations and foster trust through consistent excellence.

Innovation

We continuously seek ways to improve our products and processes, embracing new ideas to serve our community better.

Sustainability

We are dedicated to environmentally responsible practices that promote the well-being of our planet and future generations.

Community Impact

Our growth is intertwined with the well-being of our community. We aim to contribute positively through our products and practices.
